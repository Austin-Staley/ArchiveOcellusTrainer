# Mechanics status

Every number in `src/content/archive/timings.ts` is labelled unverified there. This note records the grade. Where a tick was measured, the room uses that tick. The strategy text supplies the behaviour around it.

Sources: the OSRS Wiki strategies page and the Ocellus page as of 25 Oct 2026, and the measured ledger in the research notes. A value with no measured tick is a practice stand-in and is named below.

## 3D look

The rooms are instanced, with no chunk rotation. The 3D view loads the captured template from cache 2746: the Archive Guardian is region 16471, Ocellus is 16472 on plane 1, Bork is 16727, Xarth is 16726, Moia is 16216, Ezekiel is 16214, and the Dagon'hai trio is 16215. Dreadnoth's region 16728 is data only and is not loaded. The walk grid is unchanged. The map is slid so the captured boss tile sits on the room's boss tile. Ocellus plane 1 sits 6 above the region's base, so the scene is lowered by 6 onto the walk floor. That floor overlay's 3D colour is the sentinel and it has no texture, so the room paints it with the overlay's secondary colour, the colour this cache stores for the map.

The bosses and adds use the monster ids in `timings.ts`: Bork 16640, the Smorks 16641, Ezekiel 16652, the Archive Guardian 16659 while the shield holds and 16660 once grounded, the live Guardian battery 16662, Moia 16667, undead binders 16670, Xarth 16684, the Dagon'hai trio 16643, the Hooded Figure 16672, Ocellus phase 1 16674, the Blade 16676, Ocellus phase 3 16677 (the same monster the phase 4 view already uses), the void rift 16678, the void siphon 16679, and the void augmenter 16680. Stand, walk, attack and the captured death animations come from the 25 Oct 2026 render digest. The Blade's fight actor is monster 16676. It stands still because no animation was captured for that monster. Its model is an uncoloured clickbox. The coloured sword is scenery model 62699. Object 62706 places that model once, at cache local (31, 41), and sequence 14873 hides the second sword in the same mesh. Fight log L3 has object 62706 on local (31, 41) through phase 1. It despawns on the tick npc 16676 appears, which is the phase 2 sword, so 16675 and 16676 are not in the room during the vines. The shared scene still leaves 62706 out. A death in any of these rooms restores you so the view keeps drawing, and shows the same You died, Restart and Retry seed flow as phase 4.

Hits use the same three splats as phase 4: damage, a block for zero, and a heal. A max hit uses the damage splat, because that view has no separate max-hit image. Hitsplat type 11, seen on the Dagon'hai cauldron, has no sprite here.

Each room defaults to a ranged or crush set, and Settings can switch it. Ranged is a twisted bow on rapid with Masori (f), Zaryte vambraces, Dizana's quiver, a necklace of rupture, Avernic treads (max), a venator ring and dragon arrows. ## Ocellus

Demonbane weapons deal 20% less. The wiki states this for Ocellus and for Xarth. The practice scythe is not a demonbane weapon, so the room only says so.

### Phase 1

The Hooded Figure's captured tile is trainer (5, 20), cache template (4126, 5669) plane 1, the only tile in the phase 1 captures. Npc 16672 shares models 62668 and 62669 with Ocellus 16674. The room wears 16672 for the first tick, then 16674. That is one body. There is no blade NPC in phase 1. The sword in the middle is scenery model 62699. A missed vine heals him 2. He walks the logged corners with Protect from Melee and fires magic and ranged projectiles. A volley lands 3 ticks later within 4 of his 3×3, and 4 ticks later beyond that. The animations are 14806 and 14807, with 14807 on the last volley of a walk. The projectile graphics stay 4229 and 4232. At a corner: seven vines, one every 2 ticks, each closing after 5. Standing on a closing tile costs 3 to 5 and that cost comes back when you step off. The explode pose is at C+18 and the blast one tick later, rolling 28 to 30 on every tile within 2 of his 3×3. At 66% and 25% he teleports to the middle. A cyan countdown marks where to stand, and a red outline marks the blast five ticks early. Settings can hide both.

The tendrils are the captured floor cracks: eight tiles, spotanims 4236–4259, on plane 1. They grow from the tile he is standing on. The west corners in the captures sit on column −1, outside this room, so he stops on the last tile of that path still inside it and the vines grow from there. At 66% and at 25%, read on a volley tick, he teleports out and stands in the middle four ticks later. Four beats follow, eight ticks apart. Each opens two six-tile diagonals. "Death... awaits..." is one strike of 17 to 19 when the bar is under 24. "Embrace... annihilation..." adds one shot of 17 to 19 for each 24 of that bar. That extra-shot step is the captures' engine and was never logged. The slam paints the floor blast, spotanims 4262–4265, on the tiles within 2 of his 3×3. A scorching bow can root him in the live fight; this room has no scorching bow.

The wiki's blast cap of 35, the captures' 28 to 30, and the logged 26 to 34 are **Disputed**. The room rolls 28 to 30, which is what the 182 captures and ocellus-sim 75bd193 agree on. The phase 1 drill still rolls the twelve logged hits. A missed crack heals 2. The earlier 125 heal is not used. ocellus-sim's 9 to 16 block heal is **Disputed** against the logged 3 to 5 chips; the room keeps 3 to 5 and refunds them on stepping off. Middle beats stay length 6, closing at 4 and 6, every 8 ticks from +8. He still steps one tile a tick.

### Phase 2

Orange and purple portals stand in a grid of 24 from tick 2. Orange is the gap, the colour to stand in. Purple fires, and the hit is typeless. The safe tile is where the two orange arms cross. The lit arms turn with the round, at 11, 53 and 95. The 2D floor paints the same colours. Six volleys, five ticks apart, the hit four ticks later. The volley draws projectile 4269. The fight logs name it as the portal bolt; the digest had not resolved its source. Graphic 4304, 1,262 spawns across 31 fights, plays on the sword every 3 ticks, which is the repair spotanim those logs describe. The room defaults to crush. The captures wore a twisted bow, and Settings can switch to that ranged set. The first volley is tick 11. The three orders are the verified orange → red → green cycle, running clockwise, so the first volley predicts all 18. The seed picks the starting quadrant and the first colour, because those vary and are only Likely. The quadrant is `seed % 4` over NW, NE, SE, SW, and the colour is `floor(seed / 4) % 3` over orange, red, green. A load with no seed draws a new random 32-bit seed. Seed 5 opens red in the north-east. The screen labels the current colour and quadrant. Each volley line shows the colour and both disputed letter names. The safe tile is the marked tile inside the gap, one tile in from the band the dossier drew. The purple pools and the orange pad follow the sixth volley. Stepping on the pad carries you to the next quadrant. Staying behind takes 50, an **unsourced practice stand-in** for a heavy hit, and the room says so. The phase lasts 131 ticks. Lengths of 79, 130 and 131 were all measured (**Disputed**); the five logged phase 2s end at 131. The Blade is scenery model 62699 in that model's cache colours, standing in the centre of its 3x3. He stays on that tile for the whole phase and does not follow the player. It stores damage, and the boss bar drops only when it passes on half, at ticks 46, 88 and 124. Two floors instead of three, a beam cap of 50 instead of 27, and a 2,500 hitpoint Blade are settings. The default is the better-evidenced one: three floors, cap 27, 1,000 hitpoints.

### Phase 3

You are the only player, so you are the tank. His attacks are the logged chain: the first move at 4, a swing 3 ticks later, a shadow slash 3 after that, and the next move 4 after the slash. A panic shot takes a swing's place when you are more than 2 tiles from his 3×3, lands 2 ticks later as magic for the top of 20–26 (0 under Protect from Magic), and the next move is 6 later. Two sets, then a special. Quadrant 1 opens spin, Untether, spin, Insolence. Later quadrants open Insolence, and the spin keeps alternating across a fall. The hidden drill behind `ARCHIVE_P3` uses the same chain.

The shadow slash lands 1 tick after its animation for the top of 25–28, on a 3-wide band in one of 8 directions out to the room edge. A spin's saws are at +5, +8 and +11, one saw in quadrant 1 and one more per fallen quadrant. An overhead up on the tick before a cut is severed for 3 ticks. The saw is 0 under Protect from Melee and otherwise the top of 15–27. The tick after the spin starts is a melee hit, 0 under Protect from Melee and otherwise the top of the logged 0–18. Insolence slashes at +2, +7 and +12, each landing 3 later for the top of 38–46, and swings resume at +20. Untether's slam at U+29 hits for the top of 34–39 if you are not under his 3×3. The aftershock at U+30 hits for the top of 18–41 if you are still under him. His magic at U+10, 14, 18 and 22 is 0 under Protect from Magic and otherwise the top of 20–28. The logs' unprayed magic runs 14–29, and the slam and aftershock logs run a point wider. Those wider logs are **Disputed** in the audit. Swings resume at U+37. The sim's pools at U+6, 10, 14 and 18 have no captured tile, so this room does not deal them.

Obliteration kills you 13 ticks after the shout if you are still in the quadrant, and the three rock hops fall at the jump tick, 17. During Untether the fall waits until U+31. A void vine roots you 3 ticks after the shout. It has 44 hitpoints. A solo cannot cut their own vine, and the room does not time it out.

Quadrant 1 spawns no rift. Later quadrants spawn rifts at +3, +51 and +111. The kind cycles augmenter, rift, siphon from a seed, and a living one detonates at 30 for the top of 39–41. A siphon heals him 12 or 14 every 4 ticks unless you stand on the line from the rift to his middle. Killing it does not heal you. The old heal of 22 is **Disputed** and is not applied. Killing a rift still resets your attack. Killing an augmenter sets Attack and Strength to 116 and Magic and Ranged to 112 when those stats started at 99 (**Verified**, [Void Augmenter](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Void_Augmenter), 25 Oct 2026). The same page lists 44 hitpoints. The panic shot also leaves the sim's void patch, 7 a tick for 51 ticks, which the logs do not time. The censers stay on the captured tiles and gods. Saradomin heals 50 and restores 50 prayer, as the wiki says (**Verified** as the wiki figure; **Disputed** against captures of 28–40, which the chat also names). Bandos and Armadyl call the next hit a max hit.

### Phase 4

The digest's phase 4 section is 8 fights. That sample is too small to retune the shipped loop, so the existing phase 4 fight is unchanged. The airborne half is Deflect Melee. The grounded half is Deflect Range. The loop is 60 ticks. "End..." is four ticks before the loop the ledger measured. The trainer's own cycle, counted from 1, is the one the fight still plays.

A perfect dodge halves the weapon cooldown, rounded down. Settings can switch that to cooldown minus 3. Both cut a 5-tick weapon to 2 and a 6-tick weapon to 3. A 4-tick weapon becomes 2 or 1. The strategy paste still marks "minus 3" as unconfirmed, so halved is the default. ocellus-sim 75bd193 resets that delay to at most 1. That reading is **Disputed**, and the fight does not use it. Defence reduction still stops at 60% of his level. The sim's 50% incoming factor during explosions is **Disputed** against that floor. Overhead windows stay the filmed ones: Deflect Melee from S+0 to S+24, Deflect Range from S+31 through S+52, Soul Split between them. The sim's 0–25 and 31–53, and a reference's 21 and 50, are **Disputed** against those windows. A wrong-prayer special still heals him 2 ticks later. The sim's extra claim, that any unprotected hybrid hit heals him, is flagged and not added. Orbs are ready at S+16 and S+42. The sim's +8 on the ready tick is **Disputed** and not added. Void rifts spawn on later rises, not the first. The lifeforce hit is half your current hitpoints, rounded down.

## Dreadnoth the Jellied

Dreadnoth is a Fractured Archive boss. He is the east-path third boss, the alternative to Moia. Bork and the Smorks, Dreadnoth, and Xarth are on the east side; Ezekiel, Moia and the Dagon'hai trio are on the west. The quartermaster arrives after the third boss. Sources read on 25 Oct 2026: [Dreadnoth the Jellied](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Dreadnoth_the_Jellied), [The Fractured Archive/Strategies](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/The_Fractured_Archive/Strategies), and the defence-cap note in [The Fractured Archive - Out Today](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Update:The_Fractured_Archive_-_Out_Today).

He is not a room in this trainer. The pages describe the fight and leave the tick script unspecified, so the counts below are not invented.

**Verified**

- Third boss, chosen instead of Moia. East side of the archive.
- NPC 16665, combat level 888, size 5×5, demon. Attack speed 4 ticks (2.4 seconds). Attack style melee and ranged. Max hit is blank on the page.
- Hitpoints by team size: 1 and 2 are 2,100, 3 is 3,050, 4 is 3,930, 5 is 4,810, 6 is 5,690, 8 is 7,460. The cell for team size 7 is empty.
- Defence 125. The defence-reduction table takes him to 0, and the update post lists him among the bosses with no defence cap.
- Demonbane resistance 75%. Fire spells 85% susceptibility. Freeze resistance 40%. Poison 100% resistance. Cannons immune. Thralls and burn are not immune. Venom is blank.
- He moves about one tile every two ticks and his melee hits players around him.
- At 85%, 65% and 45% of his hitpoints he does an arena-wide slam, then three ranged attacks. Each sequential slam adds a hit, up to three slams, with three ranged attacks between them. The slams also spawn jellies. The jellies attack with melee and drain prayer even when the hit is blocked.
- At 25% he says "glub glub" and jumps toward a player. Missing Protect from Melee deals damage wherever you stand. The slam heals him if it lands on a jelly, because he absorbs it.
- On death he explodes into jellies, and killing those jellies ends the encounter. The Scorching bow special can root him.

**Likely**

- "Around one tile every two ticks" is the movement line, not a measured path.
- "Roughly ten" jellies spawn when he dies.
- A failed slam heals him about 30–40.
- Jelly left on a tile deals 6–8 damage per tick. The strategies page also calls these tiles jelly, and an older copy of the same section called them blood.

**Disputed**

- Whether 85%, 65% and 45% are one slam, then two, then three, or one repeating special that escalates. The sentence supports both readings.
- The heal when he absorbs a jelly has no separate amount from the 30–40 missed-slam heal.

**Unspecified, so this trainer does not play him**

- Ticks from the hitpoint threshold to the slam, ticks between the three ranged attacks, and ticks between the extra slams.
- How many jellies spawn at 85%, 65% and 45%, and each jelly's hitpoints, size, attack speed and prayer drain.
- The melee splash radius in tiles.
- His spawn tile, and the arena's tile map.
- How often the 25% jumps repeat. The page says he begins to jump toward a player and gives no interval.
- How long the Scorching bow root lasts.
- A single puddle damage inside 6–8, and a single missed-slam heal inside 30–40.
- Team size 7 hitpoints, venom immunity, and his max hit.
