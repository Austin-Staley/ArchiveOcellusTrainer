# Mechanics status

Every number in `src/content/archive/timings.ts` is labelled unverified there. This note records the grade. Where a tick was measured, the room uses that tick. The strategy text supplies the behaviour around it.

Sources: the OSRS Wiki strategies page and the Ocellus page as of 25 Oct 2026, and the measured ledger in the research notes. A value with no measured tick is a practice stand-in and is named below.

## Ocellus

Demonbane weapons deal 20% less. The wiki states this for Ocellus and for Xarth. The practice scythe is not a demonbane weapon, so the room only says so.

### Phase 1

He walks each corner with Protect from Melee and fires magic and ranged projectiles. At the corner: seven tendrils, then a two-tile slam. Standing on a tendril blocks it. The last one heals you. A missed tendril charges him and heals him.

At 66% and at 25% he teleports to the centre, sends two tendrils clockwise four times, slams after each pair, then shouts. "Death... awaits..." is the low-meter hit of 18. "Embrace... annihilation..." uses the measured formula, 18 times the meter divided by 16. A full meter is the wiki's 80+ wipe.

The slam is 35, the wiki's cap. The ledger saw 25–40 on the same blast. The corner-to-corner walk is 10 ticks, a practice stand-in. The meter step of 16 is a practice stand-in so the formula can run. The heal after the last tendril is 10, which has no measured amount.

### Phase 2

Orange and purple portals. The safe tile is where the two orange arms cross. Six volleys, five ticks apart, the hit four ticks later. The first volley is tick 11. Three fixed patterns, in an order taken from the seed. The pad and the void pools follow the sixth volley. The Blade passes on half the damage it has stored, at ticks 46, 88 and 124. Two floors instead of three, a beam cap of 50 instead of 27, and a 2,500 hitpoint Blade are settings. The default is the better-evidenced one: three floors, cap 27, 1,000 hitpoints.

### Phase 3

You are the only player, so you are the tank. The pattern is the trainer's measured auto, auto, wave, auto, auto, wave, which sums to 20 ticks. After that cycle the charged blade spins. The first quadrant has one saw, the next two, the last three. Leaving Protect from Melee on between saws injures you.

Insolence is three waves that leave a field. Untether: step under him and leave on the final glow. This room uses four glows, the measured count. The wiki says the border glows twice. That count is still disputed.

Obliteration kills you 13 ticks after the shout if you are still in the quadrant, and the three rock hops fall at the jump tick, 17. A void vine roots a solo and they cannot cut it; it hits for 50 after 12 ticks, inside the 10–12 tick window.

Each quadrant spawns one void. A rift, a siphon, then an augmenter. If it lives 18 ticks it bursts. The 18 is inside the 15–20 tick charge and is a practice stand-in. The censers are clickable by attacking them. Saradomin heals 50 and restores 50 prayer, as the wiki says. Captures have also shown 28–40.

### Phase 4

The existing phase 4 fight is unchanged. The airborne half is Deflect Melee. The grounded half is Deflect Range. The loop is 60 ticks, the halves are 30, and the deflect windows are 21 and 19 ticks. "End..." is four ticks before the loop the ledger measured. The trainer's own cycle, counted from 1, is the one the fight still plays.

A perfect dodge halves the weapon cooldown, rounded down. Settings can switch that to cooldown minus 3. Both cut a 5-tick weapon to 2 and a 6-tick weapon to 3. A 4-tick weapon becomes 2 or 1. The wiki's "minus 3" line is still marked unconfirmed, so halved is the default.

## The other rooms

Bork drums every 7, 6, 5, then 4 ticks as the Smorks fall, and the angry chain is measured off the bomb tell. The bomb every 33 ticks is a practice stand-in inside the measured 33–88 range. Smorks defence defaults to 220, with 150 in settings. If the Smorks die while Bork lives, the room wipes you.

Ezekiel's melee is every 5 ticks and the special is every 36. The sword stuns for 8 ticks, or heals 91 if settings say the sword does not cross him. The enrage pit defaults to 33%, with 23% in settings. The safe shapes cycle three ways.

The Archive Guardian flies until the shield breaks, lands 3 ticks later, and stays grounded 35 ticks. Vines start at landing + 5 and repeat every 3 until 6 ticks before takeoff. The enrage beams default to 33% and a 10-tick travel, with 30% and 12 ticks in settings. The mushroom every 25 ticks is a practice stand-in inside 20–30.

Moia attacks every 5 ticks and relocates every 25 after the first at tick 20. She vanishes under 33% for 12 ticks, a practice stand-in inside 10–14, then chants. Eight binders at the start, six per chant. Her hitpoints default to 1,900, with 785 in settings.

Xarth fires five magic autos, then the pillar tell, then the blast. The safe tiles are the three pillar shadows, in order. After the third blast he uses ranged.

The Dagon'hai trio channels fire, earth, water, then air. Standing on the matching pad during the 5-tick window stuns them. The cauldron steps every 2 ticks, six steps, then waits out a 50-tick cycle. The book every 36 ticks is one fight's gap. Standing on the cauldron trail blocks it.

Dreadnoth is not in this trainer.
